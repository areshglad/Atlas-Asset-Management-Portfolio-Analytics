# Atlas Asset Management – Investment Portfolio Risk & Performance Analytics

## Executive Summary

Atlas Asset Management is an end-to-end investment portfolio analytics platform designed to simulate how institutional asset managers evaluate portfolio performance, risk exposure, asset allocation, diversification, and investment outcomes.

The project analyzes 10,000 investment portfolio records across multiple asset classes, sectors, investor segments, and geographic regions to generate actionable insights for portfolio managers, investment advisors, and executive leadership.

The solution combines SQL, Databricks, dbt, and Power BI to deliver a production-style analytics workflow similar to those used by leading asset management firms.

---

## Business Problem

Asset management firms oversee billions of dollars in client assets and must continuously answer critical questions:

* Which portfolios generate the highest returns?
* Which portfolios carry excessive risk?
* How diversified are client investments?
* Which sectors contribute most to portfolio performance?
* Which portfolios require rebalancing?
* How does portfolio performance compare against benchmarks?

Traditional reporting methods often rely on disconnected spreadsheets and manual analysis.

This project demonstrates how modern analytics can transform portfolio monitoring and investment decision-making.

## Key Business Metrics

### Assets Under Management (AUM)

Measures total client assets managed by the firm.

### Portfolio Return

Evaluates portfolio performance over time.

### Active Return

Measures performance relative to benchmark indices.

### Volatility

Measures portfolio risk.

### Sharpe Ratio

Evaluates risk-adjusted performance.

### Alpha

Measures excess return generated by investment decisions.

### Beta

Measures portfolio sensitivity to market movements.

### ESG Score

Evaluates sustainability and governance performance.

### Concentration Risk

Measures portfolio diversification risk.
