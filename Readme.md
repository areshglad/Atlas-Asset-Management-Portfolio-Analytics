```
Atlas Asset Management – Business Context
```

```
Atlas Asset Management is a global investment management firm responsible for
managing diversified client portfolios across equities, fixed income securities,
ETFs, alternative investments, and cash instruments. The firm serves a broad
investor base ranging from retail investors to high-net-worth and institutional
clients.
```

```
As assets under management continue to grow, senior leadership requires greater
visibility into portfolio performance, risk exposure, diversification
effectiveness, and asset allocation strategies. Portfolio managers must
continuously evaluate whether portfolios are generating sufficient returns
relative to the risks being taken while ensuring compliance with internal
diversification and concentration risk guidelines.
```

```
Management seeks an enterprise portfolio analytics platform capable of
monitoring investment performance, identifying high-risk exposures, evaluating
risk-adjusted returns, detecting concentration risk, assessing asset allocation
efficiency, and highlighting portfolios requiring rebalancing.
```

```
The objective is to support data-driven investment decisions that maximize risk-
adjusted returns, improve portfolio diversification, reduce concentration risk,
and enhance overall portfolio management effectiveness.
```

```
Stakeholders
Chief Investment Officer (CIO)
Oversees portfolio performance
Monitors firm-wide investment strategy
Portfolio Managers
Manage portfolio allocations
Evaluate performance and risk
Investment Advisors
Monitor client portfolios
Recommend portfolio adjustments
Risk Management Team
Tracks concentration risk
Monitors volatility and market exposure
Executive Leadership
Reviews AUM growth
Evaluates portfolio profitability and client retention
```

```
Business Objectives
Measure portfolio performance across all investment portfolios.
Identify top-performing and underperforming portfolios.
Monitor portfolio risk using volatility, beta, Sharpe ratio, and concentration
metrics.
Evaluate asset allocation effectiveness.
Detect portfolios requiring rebalancing.
Improve diversification across sectors, countries, and asset classes.
Support executive decision-making through portfolio intelligence reporting.
```
